Day 7 — format compliance failure (16 files)

Fifteen models ran block 01 under J-v1.1. Thirteen returned prose instead of JSON. The fleet flipped from JSON to prose in three days on an unchanged prompt. No prompt change. The shift was caught by comparing harvest outputs.

0/15 JSON fleet drift in 72 hours

This finding drove the first-character output contract and the literal template in J-v1.2. Without it, the format failure would have repeated across every subsequent wave.

What the sweep found

Zero fatal objections

No block in the stratified 10 drew a disqualifying defect at the pre-screen level. All 10 survive to expert review, now with three independent model verdicts each as context.

deepseek is the strictest reviewer

deepseek flagged SUSPECT on nine of ten blocks. kimi leaned SOUND-WITH-RESERVATIONS on nine. sonnet fell between. The divergence tracks reviewer temperament more than draft quality: all three models found real concerns on every block.

Block 02: unanimous SUSPECT

The "solved on the latest version" draft (no commit, no PR, no numbers) drew unanimous SUSPECT from all three models. The models converge on what bad evidence looks like, even when they diverge on strictness. This is the strongest consensus signal in the sweep.

Block 09: unanimous reservations

The strongest record in the set (STA false hold/setup, named fix, confirmed retest) drew SOUND-WITH-RESERVATIONS from all three models — reservations because the confirmation is the reporter's own retest, not an independent reproduction. The models correctly distinguish "I fixed it and here are the numbers" from "I fixed it, trust me."

What comes next

Sweep 1 is complete: 10 blocks × 3 models = 30 schema-compliant verdicts staged into the tally. The remaining 66 blocks (24 issue-episode + 42 paper-episode) are inventoried. The paper-episode blocks are gated on the same three-model process through J-v3, which adds two field checks for paper-recall accuracy.

After three sweeps, every block in the inventory has three independent coded verdicts. Blocks with zero fatal objections across all models graduate to the expert queue. Blocks with even one fatal are revised or retired. Blocks where the models disagree are escalated to the wider pool or flagged for the expert's direct attention.
