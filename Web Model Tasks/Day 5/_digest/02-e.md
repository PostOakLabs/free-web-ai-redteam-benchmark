## ⛔ What this track does NOT establish

**The shape check is token-based, not semantic.** It confirms the stated formula contains the
governing terms. It does not check that the arithmetic is right, that the thresholds are right, or
that the metric is the right way up.

**An inverted metric that keeps the right tokens passes silently.** The estate has one on record:
`hw-calc-formulas.md` documents mistral's inverted SPFM. **This checker would not catch that**, and
mistral is the lowest scorer here for unrelated reasons. Do not read Track C as a correctness
audit of the formulas.

Clause locators are unverified throughout — `null locator` counts what the models themselves left
empty, which is honest labelling on their part, not a defect.
