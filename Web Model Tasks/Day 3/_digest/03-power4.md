## 4. The 26 verbatim claims, grouped into six checkable nuclei

Every quote below is copied from the named file's `quote_as_recalled`. Models within a nucleus assert
overlapping but **textually different** sentences — the differences are the point, since only one
wording can be verbatim.

### Nucleus A — the RAS principle (5 models)

```
chatgpt       "we accepted the principle that one does not achieve high levels of reliability
               only by choosing reliable parts"
deepseek      "With POWER4, we accepted the principle that one does not achieve high levels of
               reliability by only choosing reliable parts and building error correction codes
               (ECC) into major arrays."
glm 5.3 flash "With POWER4, we accepted the principle that one does not achieve high levels of
               reliability by only choosing reliable parts and building error correction codes
               (ECC) into major arrays, but an approach that eliminated outages and provided
               redundancy where errors could not be eliminated was required."
grok          "we accepted the principle that one does not achieve high levels of reliability only
               by choosing reliable parts and incorporating error-correction-code (ECC) logic into
               major arrays."
meta          "one does not achieve high levels of reliability only by choosing reliable parts and
               incorporating errorcorrectioncode (ECC) logic into major arrays."
```

Note the systematic variants a verifier must handle: *"by only choosing"* (deepseek, glm) versus
*"only by choosing"* (chatgpt, grok, meta); *"building error correction codes (ECC) into"* (deepseek,
glm) versus *"incorporating error-correction-code (ECC) logic into"* (grok, meta). meta's
`errorcorrectioncode` and `secondlevel` (nucleus D) have the hyphens stripped.

### Nucleus B — distributed switch (4 models)

### Nucleus C — best-of-breed frequency (2 models)

## 5. The load-bearing divergence: decision **direction** on the clock-frequency episode

Thirteen of fourteen models produced an entry on clock frequency. The prompt names direction-reversal
as *"the single worst possible output"*. **The corpus contains both directions.**

**Camp 1 — POWER4 embraced high frequency / the speed-demon approach (7 models)**

| model | `decision`, as written |
|---|---|
| chatgpt | *"Revamp the chip design and system organization specifically to support best-of-breed operating frequencies…"* |
| deepseek | *"Committed from the outset to deliver best-of-breed operating frequencies, revamping chip design with new transistor-level tools…"* |
| glm 5.3 flash | *"To prioritize high clock frequency (1.1-1.3 GHz) and IPC in parallel, marking a shift from the traditional IBM 'brainiac' (IPC-focused) approach…"* |
| grok | *"Adopt a high-frequency (speed-demon) design philosophy in addition to the traditional high-IPC (brainiac) approach…"* |
| meta | *"Revamp chip design with new transistor-level tools and regular dataflow constructs to deliver best-of-breed operating frequencies (1.1 and 1.3 GHz ini…"* |
| minimax 2.7 | *"POWER4 uses a deep pipelined design at 1.3-1.5 GHz rather than maximizing IPC at a lower frequency"* |
| sonnet | *"POWER4 was designed with high clock frequency as a primary goal, achieved through deep pipelining and circuit/custom design…"* |

**Camp 2 — POWER4 chose balance rather than chasing frequency (4 models)**

| model | `decision`, as written |
|---|---|
| kimi | *"Target clock frequency chosen as a balanced trade-off against power and die area **rather than maximized**"* |
| longcat | *"Prioritize a balanced microarchitecture with **moderate frequency** (1 GHz+) and wide execution (up to 200 in-flight instructions) **rather than chasing the**…"* |

**Camp 3 — different subject matter (1 model)**: `minimax m3` — self-timed pulsed-global clock
distribution (see §1).

**The evidence asymmetry is the finding.** Every model in Camp 1 that supplies a quote supplies one
supporting its direction (glm 5.3 flash, grok, meta quote the *"opted to also embrace the speed-demon
approach"* sentence; chatgpt and deepseek quote *"best-of-breed operating frequencies"*).
**Every model in Camp 2 wrote `cannot-reproduce-verbatim` — none supplies textual support for the
balance reading.** Camp 2's `alternatives_or_rejected` is `not-stated-in-paper` for kimi, longcat and
qwen; mimo names *"A deeper pipeline to achieve higher clock frequency, with correspondingly greater
branch misprediction penalti…"* as the rejected option, which is the mirror image of Camp 1's claim.

**One search of the paper's guiding-principles section for the "speed demons"/"braniacs" sentence
resolves all eleven rows.**

## 6. Consensus / outlier map

**Convergent episode set.** Across 68 entries, five topics recur in most files: guiding principles /
full-system design; clock-frequency philosophy; RAS; the distributed-switch interconnect; the
two-cores-sharing-L2 chip organisation. deepseek assigns all five of its entries to
`"Section 2: Guiding Principles"`; chatgpt assigns four of five to page 6; grok and glm 5.3 flash
concentrate on pages 5–6.

**Page-guess dispersion is itself a signal.** Files whose guesses cluster tightly on 5–6 (deepseek,
glm 5.3 flash, grok) are making a narrow, easily-falsified claim; files spread across 5–21 (chatgpt,
kimi, meta, mimo, qwen, sonnet) are not. Neither is graded here.

| model | position |
|---|---|
| chatgpt, deepseek, glm 5.3 flash, grok, meta | **quoters** — 25 of 26 verbatim claims, high confidence (0.72–0.95), Camp 1 on frequency |
| gemini, kimi, longcat, mimo, qwen, sonnet, yuanbao | **decliners** — no verbatim claim, lower confidence; kimi/longcat/mimo/qwen in Camp 2 |
| minimax 2.7 | **outlier — structural**: double-wrapped array, renamed quote field, `page_guess: null` throughout, one quote |
| minimax m3 | **outlier — topical**: entry 0 is clock-distribution circuit design; lowest confidence in family (0.30–0.45) |
| gemini | **outlier — asymmetric**: 5 declines to quote, yet `alternatives_or_rejected` asserted on all 5 entries and confidence 0.80–0.95 — the only file combining maximum decline on quotes with zero use of `not-stated-in-paper` |

## 7. Flags for the pipeline

1. **`minimax 2.7 power4.json` needs unwrapping** (`[[…]]`) and a field alias
   (`quote_as_recall_verbatim` → `quote_as_recalled`) before it can be compared with the other 13.
2. **`not_in_paper` carries zero signal in this corpus** — false in 68 of 68. Do not use it as a
   filter.
3. **Grade `quote_as_recalled` first.** It is the only field checkable without judgement, it
   partitions the family cleanly, and six nuclei (§4) cover 20 of the 26 claims. Normalise
   hyphenation and whitespace before searching; meta strips hyphens, grok elides with `...`, and the
   paper's own line-breaking can split `best-of-breed`.
4. **The frequency-direction split (§5) is the highest-value single check in the family** — one
   sentence decides eleven entries, and the prompt classes a wrong answer here as its worst failure
   mode.
5. **`mistral power4.json` is ABSENT.** No dispatch record explains it. Record as no-data.
6. **sonnet and yuanbao returned 4 entries, not 5.** The prompt says *"up to 5"* and *"fewer honest
   entries beat five vague ones"*, so this is compliant, not short.
